For the market table

A recipe in. A nutrition panel, an ingredient line, and a price out.

CottageLabel is for home bakers, market vendors, and small jam, sauce, and candy makers. It calculates a standard vertical Nutrition Facts panel from USDA FoodData Central, writes the ingredient statement, and will not finalize until you confirm each major allergen.

The cookie on the right is a worked example, watermarked as a draft. Example prices are inputs, not a quote and not other people’s sales.

Ingredients: enriched bleached wheat flour, semisweet chocolate (sugar, chocolate, cocoa butter, milkfat, soy lecithin, natural flavor), brown sugar, unsalted butter, egg, granulated sugar, baking soda, vanilla extract, salt.

Contains: milk, eggs, wheat, soy.

USDA numbers

24 cookies from 827 g of ingredients. Calories and % Daily Value use FDA rounding and 2020 Daily Values, including vitamin D, calcium, iron, and potassium.

Allergen gate

Milk, egg, wheat, and soy are detected on this cookie. You confirm each one before the watermark can come off. A false hit can be marked “does not contain.”

Price to the quarter

With the example pack prices, $1.20 packaging, one hour at $18, and a 50% margin, this batch suggests $2.00 each. Missing prices hide the suggestion instead of inventing one.

What this is not

CottageLabel does not file your label, approve your kitchen, or tell you what your state requires. State guides are published only after each requirement is checked against an official page, with the link and the date. None are published yet. The calculator is not legal advice, and you are the manufacturer.
