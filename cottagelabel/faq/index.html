FAQ

Where do the nutrient numbers come from?

A cached copy of USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, April 2018, which is public domain. Each food shows its FDC id. This preview does not call the live API, so search only covers that cache. Values are per 100 grams and then scaled by the grams you enter.

Why is a nutrient marked incomplete?

Some records do not publish a value, often trans fat or added sugars. CottageLabel leaves that ingredient out of the total. It does not guess a number. You have to acknowledge the gap before a label can be finalized.

Why can’t I finalize yet?

Every detected major allergen needs a yes or no. Added-sugar foods that mix lactose or fruit sugar with sugar need a number you enter. You also confirm that you are the manufacturer. Sample data, which this cache is not, can never be finalized.

Does protein show a % Daily Value?

No. FDA expects a protein digestibility score before that percent is claimed. The panel shows protein grams only.

Will you tell me my state’s cottage-food sentence?

No. State guides are hidden until each requirement has an official source link and a verification date. None are published. You can type your own statement onto a sticker.

How do I cancel?

There is no subscription to cancel in this preview. The account page is where cancellation will live: one click, no call, and no maze. You can download your recipes from that page now.

Is this legal or nutrition advice?

No. You are the manufacturer. You are responsible for the label, the allergens, and whether your product may be sold.
