How to

  • Drag from any hole or pin to another to lay a wire.
  • Drag a part to move it (it snaps to holes).
  • Hover a hole to light up everything it's connected to.
  • Select a part or wire, then press Delete (or right-click it). Double-click a wire to remove it.
  • Board rails are drawn continuous; check whether yours split in the middle.
Hover a hole to see what it's connected to.

Pin numbers are physical header positions: DevKit V1 30-pin counter-clockwise from EN = 1; XIAO D0 = 1 … 5V = 14; module pins follow the silkscreen order. Matching net labels are connected. Power symbols: ▲ supply, ⏚ ground. NC = not connected.
