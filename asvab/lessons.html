Arithmetic Reasoning (AR)

Level 1 — What Is a Percent?

A percent means “out of 100.”
25% means 25 out of 100, which is the same as 0.25.
50% means 50 out of 100, which is the same as 0.50 or one half.

To find a percent of a number, turn the percent into a decimal and multiply.
Worked example:
What is 25% of 80?
Step 1: 25% = 0.25
Step 2: 0.25 × 80 = 20
Answer: 20
Check your understanding:
What is 10% of 50?

Level 2 — Percent Change and Averages

Percent change tells you how much something went up or down.
Formula: (difference ÷ original) × 100.

An average is the total divided by how many numbers there are.
Average example:
Scores: 78, 85, 90, and one more. The average is 82.
Total needed = 82 × 4 = 328
Known total = 78 + 85 + 90 = 253
Missing score = 328 − 253 = 75
Check: The average of 10, 20, and 30 is?

Level 3 — Work Problems

If more people work on a job, it finishes faster.
Find the total person-hours, then divide by the new number of people.
3 people finish a job in 6 hours.
Total work = 3 × 6 = 18 person-hours.
If only 2 people work, time = 18 ÷ 2 = 9 hours.

Mathematics Knowledge (MK)

Level 1 — What Is an Exponent?

An exponent tells you how many times to multiply a number by itself.
2³ means 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.
The small number (3) is the exponent. The big number (2) is the base.
Example: 3² = 3 × 3 = 9
5² = 5 × 5 = 25
2⁴ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16
Check: What is 4²?

Level 2 — Solving Simple Equations

To solve for x, do the opposite operation on both sides until x is alone.
3x + 5 = 20
Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 15
Divide both sides by 3: x = 5
Check: 3(5) + 5 = 20. Correct.
Check: Solve 2x = 10. What is x?

Level 3 — Pythagorean Theorem

In a right triangle, a² + b² = c².
c is the longest side (the hypotenuse).
A triangle with sides 3 and 4.
3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25
c = √25 = 5

Word Knowledge (WK)

Level 1 — What Is a Synonym?

A synonym is a word that means the same or almost the same as another word.
On the test you pick the word that means the same as the given word.
Abolish means to end or cancel.
Meticulous means very careful.
Arduous means hard or difficult.
Check: Which word means the same as “concise”?

Paragraph Comprehension (PC)

Level 1 — Main Idea

The main idea is what the whole paragraph is mostly about.
It is often stated in the first or last sentence.
The other sentences give details that support the main idea.
If a paragraph talks about how regular training reduces injuries, the main idea is that consistent training has benefits, including fewer injuries.
Check: The main idea is usually found where?
