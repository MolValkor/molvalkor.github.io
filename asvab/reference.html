Quick Reference Sheet

Highest-yield formulas, keywords, and strategies. Print or keep open while studying.

Arithmetic Reasoning Keywords

  • per, each, rate → divide
  • total, sum, altogether → add or multiply
  • of, percent of → multiply
  • remaining, left, difference → subtract
  • Work problems: more workers = less time (inverse)
Percent change = (difference / original) × 100
Average = sum / count
Distance = rate × time

Mathematics Knowledge Formulas

Rectangle area = l × w
Square perimeter = 4s
Circle area ≈ 3.14 × r²
Pythagorean: a² + b² = c²
Exponents: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
Difference of squares: x² − y² = (x−y)(x+y)

Electronics & Mechanical

Ohm’s Law: V = I × R
Power: P = V × I
Series: resistances add
Parallel: voltage same
Gears: opposite direction; larger = slower + more torque
Levers: longer arm = less force
