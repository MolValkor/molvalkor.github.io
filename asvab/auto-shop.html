Auto & Shop Information

Secondary subject. Knowledge of automotive systems, maintenance, tools, and shop practices. On the computer version these are separate timed sections; on paper they are combined.

Original practice questions. Not official ASVAB items. For deeper study use recommended resources.

Sample Topics to Review

  • Engine components and basic function (pistons, spark plugs, cooling system)
  • Battery, alternator, and starting system
  • Common hand tools (wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers) and their uses
  • Measurement tools (micrometer, calipers)
  • Safety practices in a shop environment

Practice questions for this area will be expanded. In the meantime, use the Resources page for video and site-based study.
