Access Options

Two monetization paths are supported. Choose the model that fits your audience and traffic goals.

Paid Access

$10–20 one-time or monthly

  • Full practice quizzes for all subjects
  • Unlimited AI Tutor sessions
  • Progress tracking and review of missed items
  • Ad-free experience
  • Downloadable study sheets (future)

Suitable for focused users who want a clean, dedicated tool. Can be implemented with a simple payment processor (Stripe, etc.) and access codes or accounts.

Free with Ads

AdSense or similar

  • Core practice and resources free
  • Limited AI Tutor messages or topic access
  • Display ads placed non-intrusively
  • Upgrade path to remove ads

Lower barrier for users. Revenue depends on traffic volume and quality (U.S. military-prep audience can perform reasonably). Start with AdSense; move to premium networks as traffic grows.

Prototype access gate: Full quiz banks, unlimited AI Tutor, and advanced features (timed mode, review queue, flashcards) can be unlocked with a one-time code or payment. Free tier includes limited practice and core lessons. Current build is fully open for testing. Integrate Stripe or similar when ready for live monetization.

Recommended Hybrid

Offer free limited practice + resources with light ads. Sell full access ($15 one-time recommended as entry point) for unlimited quizzes, AI Tutor, and ad removal. This captures both casual users and committed ones.
