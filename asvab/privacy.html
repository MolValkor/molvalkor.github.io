Privacy & Terms

Independent Study Tool

ASVAB Focus Prep is an independent educational website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Department of Defense, the official ASVAB program, or any military branch.

Data Collection

The current version stores progress, scores, ranks, and optional account details (email and phone) only in your browser’s local storage. No data is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, local progress is removed.

A future hosted version may use email for reminders and cloud sync. You will be notified before any such change.

Practice Scores

All scores, AFQT estimates, and line-score approximations are for study purposes only. They do not predict or guarantee official ASVAB results. Consult a recruiter for current enlistment requirements and job qualification.

Content

All lessons, questions, and explanations are original. External YouTube links are provided for additional learning and open in a new tab.

Use

The site is provided as-is for personal study. Do not rely on it as the sole source of preparation.
